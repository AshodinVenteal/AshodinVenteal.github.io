A simple process

Clear from hello to handoff.

  1. 1

    Share the need

    Send the goal, timing, and anything you already have. Rough notes are welcome.

  2. 2

    Confirm the plan

    I’ll recommend a scope, timeline, deliverables, and firm estimate in writing.

  3. 3

    Design together

    You’ll see work at agreed checkpoints and have space for clear, focused feedback.

  4. 4

    Receive final files

    I’ll package the approved work for its real-world use and explain what is included.

Typical timing: small requests 3–7 business days; larger projects 2–6 weeks.

Revisions: packages include two revision rounds unless noted.

Booking: projects of $250+ generally begin with a 50% deposit.
